Alphabet reference & study tips

Names are the English names used in biblical Greek study. Sigma has two lowercase forms: σ within a word, ς at the end. Both are the same letter. Learn in small groups, then switch to lowercase-only practice.

Reference: Bill Mounce’s Greek Alphabet. Alphabet names here are written labels; your device’s modern Greek audio is not used to teach biblical Greek letter sounds.
