How to study & source

Tap the card to reveal the answer. “Review again” saves the word to Missed words; “Got it” removes it. Both advance to the next card. Review is stored on this browser when storage is available. Case practice creates one card per preposition and case pairing. Chapter selection still applies.

Meanings, alternate forms, and case abbreviations are preserved. gen = genitive; dat = dative; acc = accusative. No numerical scores.
